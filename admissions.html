How to Apply

Admission, step by step

  1. Check eligibility — FA/FSc or equivalent: minimum 45% for Arts programs, 50% for Science and BBA programs.
  2. Apply online — submit the application through the university admission portal and generate your challan.
  3. Pay the fee — one processing fee covers applications to up to 3 programs.
  4. Merit lists — watch the merit lists; results-awaiting students can apply provisionally on Part-I results.
