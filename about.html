About UL · University of Layyah

Our Vision — Intention

Enlightening youth, building futures

The University of Layyah exists to enlighten the youth of the region — giving them a well-lived life along with real opportunities for entrepreneurship and employment, through modern teaching methods, practical education and collaborative research.

Our Mission — Strategy

What we are working towards

Modern Teaching

Enable students and faculty through modern teaching methods and practical, hands-on education.

Collaborative Research

Research across the spectrum of challenges and opportunities prevailing across the globe.

Skills for the Modern Era

Latest, updated and sustainability-oriented knowledge and skills to meet competitive challenges.

Goals

Our goals

  • Quality higher education for the Layyah region and beyond
  • Entrepreneurship and employment opportunities for graduates
