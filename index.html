Admissions

Apply online

Applications are submitted through the university’s online admission portal. Eligibility is FA/FSc or equivalent — minimum 45% for Arts programs and 50% for Science and BBA programs. Results-awaiting students can also apply provisionally on the basis of Part-I results.

  • Self-finance seats in computing programs
  • 5th semester intake for BA/B.Sc. holders
